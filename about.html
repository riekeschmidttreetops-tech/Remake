Who's behind it

Built by a salesperson, not a software house

I'm Rieke, and I made my first sales calls in 2014, at 19, for bonding, a student group that ran job fairs. The companies paid for everything so the fairs stayed free for students. That meant convincing them on the phone. I've kept doing it ever since, 12 years by now.

I went on to do a Master of Science in Operations Management and Research at RWTH Aachen University. I've been notoriously focused on streamlining everything ever since, and on challenging the status quo. Because only if the status quo is lifted by a mile is any investment (money, time, energy, brain capacity, status) worth anyone's time. Make data and software work for you, not you for admin.

After that I worked at Bookboon, a small Danish company that made €300K and was sold for €75M in 2022.

Somewhere in between was the fateful spring of 2020, COVID. Besides all the more important bits that came with this time, everyone was at home, worrying about yesterday, today and tomorrow. Budgets were cut everywhere, especially for digital learning, which we sold. Ours went too.

Same BDMs. No marketing budget. All we had left was the CRM, so I built the team around it. We went back to old deals, former customers and companies that had gone quiet.

ICP has been a known factor for as long as companies have existed. So has intent data. We all had LinkedIn Sales Navigator, CRM filters and reports for years, the same way everyone had the Yellow Pages and old account folders before that. I had to be smarter.

I started with CSV exports and begged for an SQL licence. I looked for dormant accounts (we first called them Sleepers) that truly had a chance to convert, based on our own history and our own product timeline. And I set up retargeting campaigns for our CEO or senior CSMs, to stop churn before it took effect.

Meetings booked went from 5 per rep a week to 15-20, and under 10 became a no-go. Most reps had 50 meetings executed a month, the best of the best even 100-150. All in our ICP. Closing rates went up, and deals closed left, right and centre.

Every other company and manager I used this approach with called the spike in (actually good) new pipe gen the "Rieke Effect". And no, none of them had a clean CRM.

We had been sitting on gold for years.

That's what ProjectSuccess does for your team. It reads your CRM and works out who each SDR and sales rep should call next, and why.
